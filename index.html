Chapter 63: Bedtime Stories

One Direction: The Labyrinth of Skull Island

The Legend: Shafu, Zayn, Edgar, and Poe walked the humble boulevard in "One Direction". Louis, driven by pride, crossed into forbidden multiple directions and became trapped in the dreaded Labyrinth of Skull Island—guarded by soul-eating Dementors!

Your Mission: Take control of Zayn and the humble crew, rescue Louis before his soul is consumed, invoke the magic word Hel-E-Cadabra, and defeat the Death Eaters with Grim Reaper's granted powers!

Game Controls & Guide

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Move Hero (Zayn)
  • Spacebar / J: Melee / Magic Staff Attack
  • Keys 1-4: Cast Spells (Hel-E-Cadabra, Soul Blast, Humble Aura, Dash)
  • E Key: Open Inventory & Equipment
  • Q Key: Open Skill Tree
  • F Key: Interact with NPCs (Grim Reaper, Shafu, Poe, Edgar, Louis)
  • Escape: Pause Menu
